04 · Chain energy requires the initial kinetic state

Distributed mass cannot be replaced by one arbitrary height. Initial kinetic energy is a separate necessary condition.

Practice: A uniform ideal chain of length 6 m, mass 3 kg starts at rest with 2 m hanging. It slides losslessly until all 6 m hang. With g = 10 m/s², find final kinetic energy. What if initial K = 5 J?

Hint 1: For hanging length h, U(h) = −(mg/(2L))h² relative to tabletop.
Hint 2: Compare U at h = 2 m and at h = 6 m; include Ki in Kf + Uf = Ki + Ui.
Uinitial = −10 J; Ufinal = −90 J, so potential falls by 80 J. Starting at rest Kfinal = 80 J; if Ki = 5 J, Kfinal = 85 J.
Conditions and review hold

Uniform inextensible chain, common speed, ideal smooth table edge, no dissipative work. Source initial-rest and contact assumptions require review; native finding OPEN.
