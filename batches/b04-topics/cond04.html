01 · Friction and force directions on a fixed incline

When a block slides on a fixed plane, weight, friction and normal reaction must be resolved separately.

Practice: A 2 kg block slides 5 m down a fixed incline with sin θ = 0.6, cos θ = 0.8, μₖ = 0.25 and g = 10 m/s². Find gravity, friction and normal work.

Hint 1: The normal reaction is mg cos θ, not mg.
Hint 2: Use Wg = mg s sin θ; Wf = −μₖ N s; WN = 0.
Wg = +60 J, N = 16 N, Wf = −20 J, WN = 0 J. Total = +40 J.
Conditions and review hold

Fixed straight incline, kinetic contact, no acceleration normal to plane, no other applied forces. R3 native finding and academic qualification remain OPEN.
